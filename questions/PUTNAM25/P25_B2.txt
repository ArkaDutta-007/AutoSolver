Putnam 2025 B2.
Let f : [0,1] -> [0, infinity) be strictly increasing and continuous. Let R be
the region bounded by x = 0, x = 1, y = 0, and y = f(x). Let x1 be the
x-coordinate of the centroid of R. Let x2 be the x-coordinate of the centroid of
the solid generated by rotating R around the x-axis. Prove that x1 < x2.
